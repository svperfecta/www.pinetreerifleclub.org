Membership Requirements

      • PTRC By Laws Article III Sections 1 and 2 Titled MEMBERSHIP
      • Must be a citizen of the United State
      • Must be at least 18 years of age.
      • Complete a membership application, available on-line and at the club house.
      • Secure a sponsor - In order to become a member of Pine Tree Rifle Club, you must be sponsored by a PTRC member.
      • Any applicant who is not an NRA member must complet an NRA membership application coincident with their application for membership to the Pine Tree Rifle Club.
      • Submit your application, with a $10 non- refundable fee to Pine Tree Rifle Club, PO Box 45, Johnstown, NY 12095
      • Upon acceptance of your application, You will be notified of a new candidates meeting and must attend with your sponsor.
