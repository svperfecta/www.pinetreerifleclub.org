Pine Tree Marksmen

Eastern New York Rifle League

The Pine Tree Marksmen shoot in the winter months (December - March) in the Eastern NY Rifle league.
We shoot on our indoor range here and travel to other clubs
and shoot indoors on Friday nights throughout the season.
We shoot according to NRA position rifle rules with target .22 rifles, scopes or iron sights.
It's lots of fun, the food is always good (even if the scores aren't!) and a good time is had by all.
We have shooters 12-18 (with supervision), adults, ladies, seniors, etc.
Everyone is welcome as long as they are a member of one of the participating clubs.
The Team is at the club every Tuesday evening for practice - join us.
