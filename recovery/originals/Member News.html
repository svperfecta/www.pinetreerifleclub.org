Pine Tree Rifle Club's newest LIFETIME Member 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Pine Tree Rifle Club Board of Directors with the clubs newest Lifetime Member GARY STOLLER (center in back row with white shirt.
 
     At the November 11th, 2009 (Veteran's Day) monthly Members meeting of the Pine Tree Rifle Club a motion was made at the meeting by the Club President Paul Catucci that Gary Stoller be bestowed with the honor of Pine Tree Rifle Club Lifetime Membership and and it was passed by the member unanimously. How fitting as Gary is a veteran who was drafted into the Army back in 1966, and did a tour of duty in Vietnam. Earning a rank of E5 and two purple hearts he was honorably discharged in 1968.
 
    Gary joined the Pine Tree Rifle Club in 1969 and has held many positions in the club where he has strived to make our club the place it is today. He also served 13 years on the clubs Board of Directors. I'm sure that you have seen him at club fundraising events and in way too many work parties to count. His effort and dedication to the club over the years has been outstanding.
 
    It should also be noted that he has been a active member of the Meco Volunteer Fire Company for 44 years. Serving his country, community, and the Pine Tree Rifle Club throughout his adult life he is an American hero. We are fortunate and proud to have him as a club member.
 
    We fellow club members of the Pine Tree Rifle Club tip our hats to Gary and say THANK YOU for all you have done for our club, community, and Country.
