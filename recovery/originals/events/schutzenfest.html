Schutzenfest 2015
 The Annual Schutzenfest and Oktoberfest was celebrated in coordination with the Concordia Club and the Pine Tree Rifle Club on Saturday, September 19. Schutzenfest directors,
Clif Wassel and Mark Wendt both agreed that the event was very successful and weather truly cooperated. Mark Duesler captured the championship followed in second place by Harold Miller,
and third place, John Quackenbush. The Schutzenfest was hosted at the Pine Tree Rifle Club and is one of many special shoots that are open to the public.

Schutzenfest 2013

  Schutzenfest 2013 was held on Saturday September 21 in near perfect conditions. The match was held in conjunction with the Concordia Singing Society's Oktoberfest celebration. Schutzenfest matches are traditionally held during Oktoberfest periods in many German towns and here in the United States.

  Fifteen shooters competed for the high desirable "Pinetree Chuck" mugs that were awarded to the top three shooters. The course of fire included first downing a bowling pin and metal plate from the off hand firing position before engaging the 100 yard woodchuck target for score. The total points possible were 120. The first place shooter Gerhard Steading had a score of 110. Second place went to Harold Miller with third place going to Blaine McRay.
