A BIT OF PTRC HISTORY RETURNS

 

It’s been sometime since I’ve put together anything for the Fireside at the Pine Tree. That hiatus was for good cause though, as much of my time was directed at research and writing a book about the history of the Pine Tree Rifle club. The book should be available August 13th at the monthly members meeting or soon after.

 

While piecing together the clubs 77 years of history several members approached me with PTRC memorabilia. Most of the items are old photos, club pins, and early membership id cards. Also there were several jacket patches as well. These are the patches that were given to the shooters who attended the benchrest matches between 1947 and 1987. They are colorful reminders of those grand rifle matches held at the club when the top shooters in the world flocked to our firing line.

 

On May 31, 2014, 36 benchrest competitors came to the firing line to take part in a registered IBS benchrest match at the PTRC. It had been 27 years earlier that the last IBS benchrest match was held at the club, and a few of the competitors made mention that they had shot on our range back then. One of them a gray haired gent handed me two old PTRC match patches, and said that he wanted to donate them back to the club. I thanked him for the patches and assured him that they would be added to the clubs archives.

 

With all the details of running the match that day I didn’t have time to take a good look at the patches till that evening. One of the patches was from the 1985 IBS International Championship Matches held at the PTRC. There were 9 different countries represented on the patch as can be seen in the present of their flags. It is rich in color and design as can be seen in the photo.

 

The second patch is from what I believe may have been the last IBS match held at the club back in 1987. That patch denotes it as the Ken Gooberlet Memorial Shoot. I remember Ken and how he loved the shooting sports and the PTRC.

 

We are starting to have a nice collection of club memorabilia, and I hope it continues to grow. It represents a physical link to our club’s history and should be persevered and displayed. At present we are limited to a small space where this done in the lounge area of the clubhouse. It is my hope that as the collections grows a proper display is established for all members to view and enjoy.

 

If you have any old photos, pins, patches, or other PTRC treasures of the club’s past I urge you to donation these items to the club. You will be helping preserve our clubs history. To donate items contact John Del Savio at the club or any member of the club’s board of directors.

 

May all your shots find their intended mark, John Del Savio
