NRA BASIC SHOTGUN SHOOTING COURSE

COURSE GOAL:To teach the basic knowledge, skills and attitude necessary for the safe and proper use of a shotgun in shooting a moving target.
Length of Course: 10 hours

Course Lessons:
    Lesson I: Shotgun Knowledge and Safe Handling
    Lesson II: Ammunition Knowledge and the Fundamentals of Shotgun Shooting
    Lesson III: Firing the First Shoot
    Lesson IV: Shotgun and Shooting Skills Practical Exercise
    Lesson V: Shotgun Games and Activities


Special Note: The purpose of this course is to teach basic shotgunning skills. It is neither designed nor intended to teach the actual sports of skeet or trap or similar activities.
