PISTOL LICENSE CLASSES


This is a required safety class for a Pistol License in NYS.
Classes to be held at the Pine Tree Rifle Club have been approved by Fulton, Montgomery,
Schenectady, Herkimer and Saratoga Counties
Instructors are NRA and PTRC certified, a number with Law Enforcement training as well.

The class will start at 6:00 p.m. on Friday night until 10:00 p.m.
On Saturday we will start again at 8:00 a.m. and it'll 4:30 with a half an hour for lunch.
The final section held Monday 6PM till complete.
Students will need to bring own lunch, water and coffee will be provided.
Program includes hands on use of inert and real pistols (indoor range).
The total cost that a class will be $175.
You can either submit full payment of $175.00
or to get your name on the list you may submit a $100.00 deposit that is non-refundable.
Submit funds to Pine Tree Rifle Club as listed below
Applicants please include a telephone number, email in case the class fills.
Class size will be limited for saftey. No refund for a No Show!
A Refund or re-schedule, only if you give 48 hour notice.
This class is accepted by Fulton, Montgomery and Schenectady counties officially.
Call your county Sheriff's Office in advance to confirm!
Counties may change their policies without notice.
