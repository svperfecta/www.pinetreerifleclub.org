Pine Tree Rifle Rifle Club Camp Scholarship Program 2020


As in previous years Pine Tree Rifle Club will Sponsor 4 children on a first come first basis, when registration starts, email paul.catucci@gmail.com and you will be email a code back to use. The parents are responsible for transportation. PTRC will cover the Camp fee. "We will send the same child no more than twice".

www.dec.ny.gov

2020 Camp registration opens at 1:00 PM on Sunday, January 26, 2020. Camp Colby is open Weeks 1-5, Camps DeBruce and Colby will be offering one program each for ages 14-17 (Week 4: July 19-24), and some of the Hunter Education program offerings have changed, so please review the schedules carefully at www.dec.ny.gov Read the Sponsor Letter (PDF) for more information.

Applications should be submitted online via the Summer Camps website.

Parents and guardians are encouraged to register early since some of the weeks fill up quickly.

For more information, please visit the DEC’s website at www.dec.ny.gov, call 518-402-8014, visit “NYS DEC Summer Camps” on Facebook, or write to DEC Camps, 3rd Floor, 625 Broadway, Albany, New York 12233-5256.
